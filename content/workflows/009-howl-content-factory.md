## Outcome

Render deterministic content assets from real Kujo examples and a manifest.

## Inputs

- showcase manifest
- Kujo examples

## Evidence and outputs

- Markdown
- HTML
- SVG
- branded social SVG
- gallery
- caption

The workflow's evidence contract is: manifest, render outputs including social variant, and status.tsv.

## Approval boundaries

- publication is outside scope

## State and recovery

timestamped generated packet

## Release boundary

Readiness is **production capable with limitations** in the 0.2.0 local technical preview. Hosted runners, broad live-provider coverage, and enterprise readiness are not implied.
