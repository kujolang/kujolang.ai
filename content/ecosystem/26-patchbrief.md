## What it does

Deterministic git-diff summaries, implementation handoffs, changed-file risk notes, reviewer context, and suggested tests.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Make a code change easier to review and easier to hand off.

## Operating boundary

PatchBrief summarizes evidence from a diff; it does not replace reading sensitive or high-risk changes.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.
