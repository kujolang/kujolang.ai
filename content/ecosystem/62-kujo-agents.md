## What it does

Reusable agent packages and chain-of-command roles with tool mappings, responsibilities, escalation paths, and ProofPack conventions.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Make delegation, verification, and handoff responsibilities explicit.

## Operating boundary

Agent roles are operating contracts, not independent authority to mutate systems or make organizational decisions.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.
