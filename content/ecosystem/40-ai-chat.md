## What it does

A local multi-provider chat application with encrypted provider profiles, SQLite history, streaming, comparison, transcription, and fixture smokes.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Demonstrate provider-gated AI application boundaries in a complete, inspectable local app.

## Operating boundary

AI Chat 1.2.0 adds durable recovery and documentation-aware chat. Use Node 22.17.0, npm ci, and database/encryption backups. The eight-hour soak remains incomplete; browser execution requires supported OS containment.
