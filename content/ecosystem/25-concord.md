## What it does

Cross-artifact drift checks for code, CLI behavior, docs, examples, specs, evals, schemas, manifests, and sources of truth.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Find when the ecosystem's explanations and contracts stop agreeing with its implementation.

## Operating boundary

Concord is an early dogfood tool; findings still require project-aware triage.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.

## October 14 release update

Six native checks and fixes for drift false positives, reports, CLI/runtime behavior and bounded traversal improve artifact alignment.
