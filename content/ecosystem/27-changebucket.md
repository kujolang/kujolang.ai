## What it does

Change-footprint and blast-radius reports covering churn, categories, generated files, CI, deletions, dependencies, and budgets.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Detect scope expansion before a focused change becomes an unreviewable patch.

## Operating boundary

Budgets are policy signals, not proof that a small change is safe or a large change is wrong.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.
