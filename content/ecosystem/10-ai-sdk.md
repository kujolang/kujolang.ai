## What it does

Provider-normalized chat and embeddings contracts with presets, fixtures, retries, streaming, redaction, budgets, and telemetry hooks.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

A provider-gated foundation for AI calls without binding application code to one hosted service.

## Operating boundary

Live-provider behavior remains an explicit integration boundary; deterministic fixtures support offline verification.
