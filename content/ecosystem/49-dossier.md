## What it does

Dossier gives editors, agents, CI jobs, and review workflows a portable record of what a material claim may honestly rely on. It links claims to exact source locations, captured support, checksums, retrieval times, reviewers, conflicts, quotations, consent assertions, and rights assertions.

## Explicit evidence states

Claims distinguish facts, observations, inferences, opinions, and hypotheses. Evidence can be verified, observed, inferred, planned, conflicted, expired, unavailable, rejected, or otherwise explicitly classified, preventing weak support from being presented as settled fact.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Publishing work becomes safer when provenance survives handoffs. Dossier makes the standards desk's reasoning portable and inspectable without pretending that storage alone creates truth or authority.

## Operating boundary

Records are immutable, history is append-only, and exports are bounded and atomic. Verification requires exact captured evidence; consent, rights, and approval remain scoped human and legal determinations.
