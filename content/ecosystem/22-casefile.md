## What it does

Reproducible failure and incident bundles with provenance, redaction, logs, reproduction notes, JSON, Markdown, and handoffs.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Package a failure so another person or agent can reproduce and continue the investigation.

## Operating boundary

Sensitive inputs still require review; redaction reduces risk but does not replace data-handling policy.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.
