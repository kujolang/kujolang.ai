## What it does

Resumable multi-step workflows with templates, retries, approvals, persisted state, traces, reports, policy controls, and signed bundles.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Auditable workflow orchestration for missions that must pause, resume, and preserve operator control.

## Operating boundary

The local and offline orchestration path is primary; live integrations need environment-specific validation.
