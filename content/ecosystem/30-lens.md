## What it does

Configured browser checks, screenshots, console and network evidence, accessibility findings, visual baselines, flows, and repair briefs. Results depend on browser, fonts, timing, data and network conditions.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Produce browser-backed proof that a UI behaves as intended.

## Operating boundary

Lens is beta and stabilizing; automated evidence complements rather than replaces representative human review.

## Learn more

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.

## Inspect the evidence

Review the [published release](https://github.com/kujolang/lens/releases/tag/v1.1.1), [source and test instructions](https://github.com/kujolang/lens), and [agent accountability boundaries](/agent-accountability/). Run the documented checks in your own environment; release labels do not certify every provider, host or workload.
