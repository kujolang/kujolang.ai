## What it does

Durable receipts for tasks, models, providers, git state, changed files, usage, cost, verdicts, follow-ups, comparisons, and reports.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Preserve proof of agent and build runs so later review starts from evidence.

## Operating boundary

Receipts record declared and observable run data; they do not independently certify correctness.

The repository is the source of truth for current setup, commands, examples, security notes, compatibility, and verification evidence.
