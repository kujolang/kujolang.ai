## What it does

BluePencil combines deterministic contract checks with independent human or optional-model judgment across consequence, distinctiveness, insight, defensibility, craft, brand integrity, format fidelity, and strategic purpose. Reviews retain rubric and reviewer identity, findings, blockers, and an explicit verdict.

## Calibrated review

The repository includes an 18-case blind calibration corpus covering all 23 Publishing House roles. Operators can compare immutable reviews, preserve disagreement, and inspect focused style, brand, claims, format, and accessibility findings without flattening them into a misleading average.

## Why it belongs in Kujo

Editorial quality depends on judgment that remains attributable and reviewable. BluePencil gives agents and people a shared evidence format while keeping taste, accountability, and publication authority with the responsible humans.

## Operating boundary

Blocking findings take precedence over aggregate ratings. BluePencil proposes and records; it does not silently edit source work, resolve disagreement, or grant approval.
