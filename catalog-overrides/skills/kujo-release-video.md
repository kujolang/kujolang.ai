## A focused release announcement

Turn inspected release notes, a changelog, a local diff, or a release URL into a 15-second, 1920×1080, 24 fps monochrome film. The bundled trust-at-speed preset includes a release-specific script, ElevenLabs narration, original ambient music, and synchronized sound effects.

```text
Use $kujo-release-video to make a release film for [tool/version] from
[release information], in a new folder. Generate the ElevenLabs narration
and use the original ambient music and sound effects workflow.
```

Brian is the default narrator; an explicit user voice choice takes precedence. Rebuilds reuse matching takes. Provider credentials, available allowance, authorization, and generation-time usage rights still apply.

Use [Kujo Video Styles](/ecosystem/skills/kujo-video-styles/) for longer films, product demos, launches, explainers, other story structures, or different aspect ratios. Do not stretch the fixed preset's timing to fit another style.

[Install the video skills and follow the setup guide](https://docs.kujolang.ai/collections/video-skills/).
