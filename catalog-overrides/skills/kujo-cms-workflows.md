## CMS release capabilities

CMS 1.2.0 includes framework-neutral sessions and capabilities, granular SEO and social operations, verified extension ZIP ingestion, plugin abilities and connectors, atomic content and media workflows, official JavaScript and PHP clients, and built-in WebMCP. Mutating Abilities require request-bound one-time approvals. The release also tightens expiry, media rate limits, extension cleanup, content conflict checks, and queue claims. Read the [1.2.0 upgrade notes](https://github.com/kujolang/cms/blob/main/docs/releases/1.2.0.md) before deployment.
