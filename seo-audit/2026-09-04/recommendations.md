# Recommendations and measurement plan

Audit date: 2026-09-04

## Immediate after deployment

- Repeat the four production Lighthouse templates and confirm that animation scripting/TBT falls.
- Resolve the private-repository/public-CTA decision and re-probe the six affected links.
- Add responsive catalog image derivatives and `srcset`/`sizes`; compare `/ecosystem/` image bytes.

## 7-day checks

- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; record discovered/indexed counts and exclusions.
- Review CDN logs for crawler access, 404s, and 5xx responses if access becomes available.
- Establish field performance through CrUX or privacy-conscious first-party RUM.

## 28-, 60-, and 90-day comparisons

Repeat identical queries, controlled AI-answer questions, coverage, clicks/impressions, referring domains, and template field CWV with consistent country/device settings. Do not infer causation from correlation.

## Editorial decisions

Publish evidence-led pages only for publicly usable projects. Keep PackWrite centered on its concrete workflow and reproducible commands. Treat IndexNow as a measured notification experiment, never an indexing promise.
