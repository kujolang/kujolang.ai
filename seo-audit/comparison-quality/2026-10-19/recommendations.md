# Follow-up measurement plan

Immediately after deployment: verify the eight canonical routes, social-image hashes, metadata, sitemap/robots, Markdown parity, citations and genuine 404s. Keep the report and source commit together.

After 7 days: with Search Console and Bing access, inspect the eight URLs' indexing/canonical status and sitemap processing. Check request logs for real crawler responses. Review any substantive language release using fact-review-prompt.md.

After 28 days: compare equal 28-day windows for these same pages and query themes. Record impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, country/device, documentation/GitHub/install clicks and identifiable AI referrals. Do not call unattributed traffic AI traffic.

After 60 and 90 days: repeat equal-window comparisons; account for release dates, seasonality and sitewide changes. Repeat the fixed questions in ai-search-benchmark.csv on named providers with model/date/locale, save raw answers and citations, and assess representation accuracy. Do not collapse mentions and citations into one measure.

When performance tooling is available, run repeatable mobile and desktop Lighthouse trials for the hub, one direct comparison and one guide, retaining raw reports and median results; compare field CWV separately. Static asset sizes in this audit are only a footprint baseline.

No scheduled jobs, paid services or crawler-policy changes were created. The next factual review should verify stable versus preview releases and preserve uncertainty instead of replacing it with marketing certainty.
